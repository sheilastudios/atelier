# Atelier: Candidate Work, Provenance and Review

Atelier is a Sheila Studios workbench project, presented here as a source-inspected prototype design account. This is not a demonstration of an operating workflow or a ready-to-use product.

Collaborative work produces more than finished answers: drafts, alternatives, revisions, review comments and material that has been replaced but still needs to be understood. A workbench must help people distinguish something that is merely present from something current enough to consider. Recording that distinction and enforcing it wherever material is used are different engineering tasks.

Atelier explores this problem through candidate-work records, provenance, successor relationships and review information. Its particular interest is the lifecycle of work between creation and adoption, rather than the generation of another answer.

## What the inspected source shows

**Version-sensitive updates and provenance.** One inspected update path compares versions and records changes. That is a concrete implementation observation, but it does not demonstrate uniform protection across the application, authenticated authority or an immutable history.

**Successors and exclusion information.** Source represents relationships between earlier work and its successor, including information that marks material as excluded from further use. The presence or display of that information does not establish that every consumer honors it, or that publication is atomic.

**Review information and blockers.** Review metadata and blocker annotations make another distinction visible: a system can record a reason to withhold work without demonstrating an end-to-end gate that prevents its use. The inspected material supports an account of those representations, not a claim of universally enforced review.

Together, these observations make Atelier a useful design case: how far does a declared lifecycle actually reach? The source contains part of an answer, while the limits identify what a stronger operational demonstration would still need to establish. Stored checksums, for example, should not be mistaken for independently verified artifacts.

## Relationship to Infinite Manuscript

Historical planning positioned Infinite Manuscript as a creative use case within Atelier. They remain distinct projects. Infinite Manuscript's separately evidenced narrative and visual work does not establish Atelier's readiness, nor does this account imply that its current implementation runs inside Atelier.

## Scope of this account

This account rests on a bounded inspection of selected prototype source and design records, not a complete build or current runtime audit. It includes no new execution, browser demonstration, performance measurement or claim of deployed use. Missing results within that inspection are not evidence that no results exist elsewhere.

See [Evidence and Limits](EVIDENCE.md) for the distinctions that govern these claims. No installation instructions, source download or service offer accompanies this design account.
