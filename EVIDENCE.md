# Evidence and Limits

This is a source-only account of selected Atelier prototype material. Implementation observations come from Arden's bounded static inspection, with subsequent editorial review by Sheila. Editorial acceptance is not an independent replication of the code inspection or a product test.

## Claim Boundaries

| Observation | Evidence level | What it does not establish |
|---|---|---|
| An inspected update path represents version-sensitive changes and provenance. | Static implementation observation. | Uniform protection, authenticated authority, immutable history or concurrent correctness throughout the application. |
| Source represents successor relationships and exclusion information. | Static backend and interface observations. | Enforcement by every consumer, a demonstrated browser workflow or atomic publication. |
| Review information and blocker annotations are represented. | Static observation supporting a design analysis. | End-to-end approval or admission enforcement. |

Historical plans and pressure reviews establish design intent and the proposed relationship to Infinite Manuscript. They do not establish deployment or transfer Infinite Manuscript's later results to Atelier.

## Inspection Scope

The documentary baseline consists of selected original prototype files and bounded inspected portions, not a complete source snapshot or a selected runnable release. A separate fixes variant was retained as contrast evidence, not silently merged into this account. A match between individual files does not establish equivalence between whole applications.

No new builds, tests, browser sessions or model evaluations were performed for this account. No retained operational result was located within the bounded inspection; no exhaustive search is claimed. Static limitations are not presented as newly executed failures, and historical repair notes do not establish a current successful build.

A stronger operational account would require a complete selected edition and version-bound evidence of the relevant workflow, including failures and the actual consuming paths. A build pass alone would not establish lifecycle enforcement. That evidence is not supplied here.

Private source bindings and review records remain outside these reader-facing documents. No private code, formats, prompts, manuscripts or operational data are released by this account.
